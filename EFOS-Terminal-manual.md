# EFOS

TERMINAL \>\_

Manual for Ableton Move (Schwung module), version 0.118.172 (26 November 2026)

![EFOS Terminal on the Move: pads, knobs and buttons](device.png)

EFOS Terminal is a generative instrument for the Ableton Move layout: eight tracks that write their own rhythms, melodies and sounds, and that you steer, mutate and perform. It runs on the Move as a Schwung module and takes over the whole Move: screen, pads, knobs and buttons.

## 1 The Move

EFOS Terminal takes over the whole Move. Below are the Move's buttons and what they do in EFOS; the screen and the rest of this manual use the EFOS names in CAPS.

Turning a step off with a tap also clears its P-LOCKs (UNDO brings them back). ## 2 Keys and pages

Four keys on the left, each with its own pages. Page 1 of every key is the page you play on; the pages after it go deeper.

| Key | Pages (+ / −) | For |
| TRIGGERS | TRIGGERS, TRIGGERS 2, STUTTER, STUT.LFO | When a track plays: algorithm, rotate, density, probability, variation, length; clock, listen to another track, repeat; stutter and repeat. |
| PITCH | PITCH, PITCH 2 | What it plays: pitch, range, follow and harmony, glide, melody variation; scale, root, detune, Turing. |
| ENGINE | ENGINE, ENGINE 2, MOD, FX SLOTS, SIDECHAIN, CHANNEL | How it sounds: the shape of the sound (ENGINE), its algorithm and nature (ENGINE 2), modulation, the two effect slots and the channel strip of the track. |
| PERF | PERFORM, PERFORM 2, SEND A, SEND B, COMP, ALL ENGINE, ALL TRIGGERS | Playing live over everything: breaks, chaos, filter, swing, sends, master compressor, mutate, default; one parameter across all tracks. |

**Names:** a knob name gives the part it belongs to first, then what it does: F.ATK = the attack of the filter envelope, C.DEC = the decay of the click, STUT.P = the stutter PROB. Where the header already names the part, the name is a whole word (SIDECHAIN: AMOUNT, TIME, SOURCE). Families keep their name: VAR.T = variation of the triggers, LFO1.S = speed of LFO1. VAR is a little change every loop; RND (on the pads) is something new at once. Touch a knob to read its full name.

**Option knobs:** a knob with fewer than 15 options does not speed up, and each option holds for a few clicks so you do not shoot past it: 8 clicks with 2 or 3 options, 6 with 4, 5 with 5, down to 3 with 8 or more. A knob that picks an option (ALGO, MODE, HARM, VOICE, SCALE, CLOCK, LIST.T, LOOP.N, ROLL.L, CHORUS and the like) shows a short line on what the picked option does at the bottom of the screen while you touch it, for example A: NOTES RUN ON EVERY STEP. **Lists** longer than the screen (MENU, PRESETS, ENGINE, the file lists) have a scrollbar on the right: the block shows where you are and how long the list is.

**SHIFT on a knob:** where a knob has its own SHIFT function (ATTACK, COLOR, PAN and so on), SHIFT gives that. Every other knob of a track sets the same value on all eight tracks while SHIFT is held; the screen says ALL TRACKS. On an FX slot it goes to every track with the same effect in that slot.

The header shows the selected track, its engine and its length, and on the right the page number (for example 1/2). On PERF the header can show the master length and the CPU load of the engine, updated twice a second.

### REC: motion recording

Press REC so it lights, and press PLAY. Turning a knob now records its value on the steps that pass while you turn; on playback the knob follows the recording on those steps, and steps without a recorded value play the knob where it is. Every knob that shapes the sound can be recorded, also under SHIFT: ENGINE (also the newer knobs such as the SAMPLE knobs, SIDECHAIN and CHANNEL), MOD, the FX slots, the send amounts, PROB and PAN on a track; the send knobs, PERFORM, the compressor and STUTTER for all tracks. Knobs that build the pattern (ALGO, ROTATE, LENGTH, CLOCK, DENS, the VAR knobs, L.TRIG, RPT.T, pitch MODE) and the send TYPE are not recorded.

| Track recordings | Run on the steps of that track (up to 64), up to 16 knobs per track. |
| Global recordings | Sends, PERFORM and compressor run on the master clock (STUTTER knobs are per track and record on the track): the master length, or 4 bars when it is off. Up to 16 knobs. |
| Indicator | A knob with a recording shows a small dot at the top right of its name on the screen, and the light under the knob on the Move blinks. The light burns steadily when an LFO or random modulation moves that knob; with both, it blinks. |
| Clear one knob | Hold DEL and turn that knob a little: its REC recording and its step P-locks go, on every knob. A small dot at the top right of a knob cell shows it has a recording or P-locks. On ENGINE this also removes its modulation links; the screen says what was cleared. |
| Clear a page | SHIFT + REC: on a track page the recordings of that track, on PERF the global recordings. |

Press REC again to stop recording; the recording keeps playing. UNDO brings back what you cleared. Recordings are saved in presets.

## 3 Tracks: select, mute, solo

Pad row 1 works the same on every page. The selected track's pad is white. Press a pad in rows 2–4 that has no function and no track is selected: the white light goes out, the screen shows ---- instead of the track, and the knobs do nothing, so nothing turns by accident; only SHIFT + a knob that sets all tracks still works. A pad in row 1 selects a track again. M has two states: off, or on (M lit). Tap M to switch; holding M also counts as on while you hold it.

| | M off (normal) | M on (M lit) |
| Tap a track | select track | mute on / off |
| SHIFT + tap | mute on / off | solo: only this track |

- **Group mute:** hold SHIFT and M together and tap tracks. They blink; when you let go of SHIFT or M they all flip at once.
- **Solo** is one track at a time. Do it again on the same track to end it: then all tracks play, also after you added tracks to the solo or muted others. A soloed track always sounds, also if it was muted. Silenced tracks pulse.
- **Muting while a solo is on** first turns the solo into plain mutes (you hear the same), then flips the mute, so a mute always does what it says.
- A track flashes white on every hit.

## 4 TRIGGERS

### TRIGGERS: when a track plays

TRIGGERS has two pages of knobs (press TRIGGERS again for the next page). SHIFT + any knob on them = the same on all tracks.

| ALGO | How the rhythm is made: EUCL (Euclidean: the hits spread as evenly as they can), TURING (a Turing machine: the rhythm changes by itself, see knob 2), ALG.G (algorithm grooves, a library of 16 grooves: FOUR, BACK, OFF, 8THS, 16THS, TRESIL, CLAVE, RUMBA, BOSSA, CINQ, DEMBOW, BREAK, 2STEP, SHUFFL, GALLOP, AFRO) ROLL (an even base rhythm like EUCL, with now and then a run of 16ths in a row on top: handy for a quick double kick or a burst of bass notes) or OTOMAT (the track plays on the wall hits of the shared OTOMAT field, see OTOMAT below; its own pattern is not used). Going back to an earlier algorithm brings its pattern back. |
| Knob 2 | Follows ALGO. With TURING it is TURING: 0 = locked; turned up, every step that has just played may become a new hit or rest, at the density; fully up, every round is new. Back to 0 locks what you hear. LINK (PITCH 2) makes this and the melody TURING one machine. With ALG.G it is GROOVE: picks the groove. With ROLL it is ROLL.L: how many 16ths in a row (2–16). With EUCL it is empty (----). With OTOMAT knobs 2–4 are CELL, COL and ROW (see OTOMAT below). |
| DENS | Density: how many hits. With ALG.G the middle plays the groove as written; to the left only the heaviest hits stay, to the right ghost notes come in. With ROLL, DENS sets the hits of the even base rhythm; SHIFT + DENS = ROLL.D, how often a run comes (0 = no runs, otherwise at least one), for this track only. |
| ROTATE | Turns the pattern step by step, to the right when you turn right, with every ALGO. With EUCL the pattern is made again rotated; with the others the steps there are move along (steps you drew move too). The rotation stays when DENS, GROOVE or ROLL.L makes a new pattern. |
| PROB | Probability per step. |
| VAR.T | How much the rhythm changes each loop: hits of your pattern move one step or drop out, a new hit never appears (DENS 0 stays silent). It plays a variation on your pattern and never overwrites it: back to 0 = exactly your pattern. |
| VAR.V | Velocity variation (accents): 0–64 depth, 64–127 less and less locked. |
| LENGTH | Track length 1–128 (8 step pages of 16). Turned slowly it moves one step per 2 clicks, so every length is easy to land on; turned fast it jumps further, as far as the knob sends. On 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 and 128 it always stops (also a fast turn never jumps over them) and holds for 5 more clicks; turned slowly it also holds on 3, 6, 12, 24, 48 and 96 for 2 clicks. A new length keeps your pattern: shorter hides the last steps (longer again brings them back), longer than ever before repeats the pattern into the new steps. |

### TRIGGERS 2

Knob 1 CLOCK, knob 2 LIST.M, knob 3 LIST.T (on knob 3 like LIST.N on PITCH), knob 4 LOOP.N.

| CLOCK | Clock divider /8 /4 /3 /2 1 X2 X3 X4, locked to the master clock: after a change, also one made by MOD, the next step waits for the beat grid of the new clock. |
| LIST.M | Listen mode for LIST.T: POST (default) = the track only follows the source's hits that pass the source's DENS and PROB, also while the source is muted; its stutters and repeats are not passed on. PRE = every pattern step of the source, before its PROB. The listening track's own LIST PROB (SHIFT + PROB) still decides on top. SHIFT = all tracks. |
| LIST.T | Listen triggers: also play on the hits of another track (OFF, T1–T8): for example a bass that hears the kick plays on the kick's hits as well as on its own. SHIFT = every track hears the same track as the selected one; that track itself goes OFF. |
| LOOP.N | Loops the last 4, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 24, 28, 32, 64, 96 or 128 steps of the track as you heard them, stutters and glitches included; all the way left = OFF. Only while playing. |

Pads, row 2 (SHIFT = all tracks; every press shows what it did on the screen). Colours: VAR.1 and V1.INT orange, VAR.2 and V2.INT green, FILL pink; bright = on. VAR.1 and VAR.2 are fixed loops made from your triggers, so your pattern is never lost; change it and they follow. Both may run at once. Unlike VAR.T, which changes the triggers a little every loop.

| Pad | What it does |
| 1 VAR.1 | Each press one step on: OFF (as written), then 4 variants with as many hits as your rhythm (the downbeat stays, some other hits move to a free step nearby), back to OFF. |
| 2 VAR.2 | On / off. Every press that turns it on makes a new random fill: free 16ths are added, more and more towards the end of each bar, now and then a short run of 16ths. The added hits are ghost notes, played softer; your own hits stay. |
| 3 V1.INT | How often VAR.1 plays: OFF, EVERY BAR (VAR.1 all the time), EVERY 2, 3 or 4 BARS (then only the last bar of every 2, 3 or 4 plays VAR.1, the rest as written). Each press steps on; after 4 comes OFF. A bar = 16 steps, counted from PLAY. The pad is lit while VAR.1 is on. |
| 4 V2.INT | The same for VAR.2: EVERY 2 BARS plays the fill on the second bar, EVERY 4 BARS on the fourth, so the fill comes at the end of every 2, 3 or 4 bars. |
| 5 FILL | Hold: the steps with the trig condition FILL play, the steps with NOT FILL rest (see TRIG CONDITIONS). SHIFT + hold = all tracks. |
| 7 TURING+, 8 TURING− | A hit with a new note on the next step, or a rest there, like the write switch of a Turing machine (hold = on every step while held). |

Row 3 (purple) shapes a held repeat: pads 1–4 RISE, the repeat swells in from silence over 1/4, 1/2, 1 or 2 bars; pads 5–8 SWEEP, after the RISE the stutter's LFO DEST (for example VOLUME or LP CLOSE) moves from 0 to full over 1/2, 1, 2 or 4 bars and stays there until you let go. One of each can be on; the lit pad again = off. On the stutters of PROB, RISE and SWEEP work too, within each stutter: RISE over 1/8, 1/4, 3/8 or 1/2 of it, then SWEEP over 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 or all of the rest. While you hold a REPEAT pad, a row 3 pad starts the RISE and SWEEP again with its time. Row 4 (blue): REPEAT 1/32, 1/16, 1/8, 3/16, 1/4, 1/2, 1 bar or REVERSE of the selected track, while you hold the pad; it starts on the 16th. Pads 1–7 are a note repeat: the engine of the track is struck again at that rate (its own pattern waits while you hold), so every repeat is a fresh hit and effect tails ring on; RISE is the velocity of the strikes, SWEEP and the LFO move their DEST on the track (not PITCH DOWN / UP), RAMP, BREAK (skip) and VAR.C change the strikes. Pad 8 REVERSE plays the audio of the last step backwards. SHIFT = all tracks (the master repeat, which repeats the audio of the whole mix). While you hold a repeat pad, tap row 1 pads to add or drop tracks; with M on (or MUTE held) row 1 mutes and unmutes tracks instead, as always. Step buttons set or clear steps.

### OTOMAT

A generative sequencer after Otomata by Batuhan Bozkurt (2011), built from its rules (own code). There is one field of 8 × 8 squares, shared by all tracks: each column belongs to a track (T1 left to T8 right) and so does each row (T1 top to T8 bottom). On the field are cells, each moving UP, RIGHT, DOWN or LEFT. Every 16th each cell moves one square. A cell that hits a wall turns around and plays a hit: a cell moving up or down plays the track of its column, a cell moving left or right the track of its row. When two or more cells land on the same square, each of them turns a quarter clockwise; so the pattern keeps changing slowly without you doing anything.

Set ALGO (TRIGGERS, knob 1) to OTOMAT on every track that should listen to the field; the other tracks keep their own pattern. A track on OTOMAT does not play its own steps: the step buttons show where the field played it this loop. The first time, the field starts with 5 cells, and a track that turns OTOMAT while no cell plays it gets a cell of its own (moving up in its column). On TRIGGERS page 1 knobs 2–4 then follow the ALGO, as knob 2 does for the others; knobs 5–8 stay. While you touch knob 2, 3 or 4 the lower half of the screen shows the field: each cell is a dot with a tick on the side it moves to, the square you edit is a filled square.

| CELL | Knob 2: the cell on the square you edit: ---- (none), UP, RIGHT, DOWN, LEFT. UNDO takes it back. |
| RND.F | SHIFT + knob 2: a new random field with this many cells (0 = empty, up to 16); the value shows how many cells there are now. |
| COL | Knob 3: the column of the square you edit (T1–T8). |
| ROW | Knob 4: the row of the square you edit (T1–T8). |

PLAY from the start puts the field back where it was when you last changed it, so a song starts the same each time. The field is saved with the preset.

### STUTTER

The pads are the same as on TRIGGERS. There are no stutter pads: you stutter with the knobs of this page and PROB. A stutter or repeat starts on the next 16th and catches a hit you just missed. The knobs belong to the selected track; SHIFT + turn sets that knob for all tracks and for the master repeat.

| PROB | The chance that a hit of the track starts a stutter of that hit, 2, 4 or 8 16ths long: with LENGTH 1/16 and RAMP, BREAK and VAR.C off you hear the hit retriggered on every 16th. At 100 % every hit that falls outside a running stutter starts one. A muted track does not stutter. |
| LENGTH | The length of one repeat, 1/128 to 1 bar. |
| RAMP | The stutter speeds up in steps: 0 = off, then 1, 2 or 3 steps, each halving the repeat (1/16, 1/32, 1/64), spread evenly over the stutter and always on the grid. Never faster than 1/64. Works on held stutters (over one bar) and the ones PROB starts. |
| BREAK | Now and then a repeat is reversed or skipped (only RAMP changes the length). |
| SHAPE | The shape of each repeat: left open (the repeat sounds in full), right tight (a short strike that dies at once). |
| VAR.C | Variation of the stutter clock, 0 (off) in the middle. Left = clock divider: now and then a repeat plays and the next 1 (or 3) stay silent, a slower clock. Right = clock multiplier: now and then a repeat splits into 2 (or 4) shorter ones. Further from the middle more often, and from about 70 % also /4 and x4. Always on the grid. |
| LFO | One LFO over the stutter: how far it moves its destination. Its SPEED and DEST are on the next page, STUT.LFO. SHIFT = all tracks, as every knob of this page. |
| MIX | Stutter mix. |

**STUT.LFO** (+ after STUTTER): SPEED = the speed of the stutter LFO: at the left end one cycle per repeat (it follows LENGTH), further right 1 bar down to 1/64 on the clock. DEST = its destination (and where SWEEP goes): LP OPEN, LP CLOSE, HP OPEN, BP SWEEP, PITCH DOWN, PITCH UP, VOLUME, PAN. SHIFT + turn = all tracks and the master repeat.

Destinations for the LFO: LP OPEN, LP CLOSE, HP OPEN, BP SWEEP, PITCH DOWN, PITCH UP, VOLUME, PAN. ## 6 ENGINE

### ENGINE and ENGINE 2

**PLAY pad:** on every ENGINE page except FX SLOTS, row 3 pad 3 is a green PLAY pad: one hit of the selected track, also when stopped. A track with RANGE on plays its ROOT note (the MAIN ROOT when MAIN is on); a track with RANGE 0 plays at its own PITCH. On SAMPLE it plays one hit from START on every press too (never start / stop); after a new take of the Sample button the first press loads the take. **Hold a step** and press PLAY: you hear that step as the sequencer plays it, with its P-locks (for example a START per step on SAMPLE), its note and its velocity (a VAR.2 step as a ghost note); this works on every engine, SAMPLE too. The held step itself stays on or off as it was. The step's values stay until the next step plays or the next PLAY without a held step.

Page 1 (ENGINE) holds the shape of the sound and the knobs you play most, in the same places on every engine: the ones you reach for first on 1–3 (FILTER or CUTOFF, then P.ENV, F.ENV or DRIVE, then VAR.S), the envelope on 4–6, VAR.D on 7 and LEVEL on 8. RND.S and the N key also give the SHIFT jobs a new value. A knob without a job shows ----. While you hold SHIFT the header says ALL TRACKS (ENGINE 2: ALL and the engine name). ### FX SLOTS

Two kinds of effects, as on a mixing desk. **Slots** are insert effects: two per track, the track's sound goes through slot 1 and then slot 2, and they belong to that track only. The stereo width of a GRAIN or SPRING in slot 1 also goes through slot 2 when that is FILTER, DRIVE, CRUSH or RING (left and right each through it); after a DELAY, RESO, GRAIN or SPRING in slot 2 the width of slot 1 is added as it is. **Sends** (A and B, on PERF) are two shared effects: one of each for all tracks, and every track chooses how much it sends (ENGINE row 4). So at most four effects work on a track: its two slots and the two sends.

The top row of knobs (1–4) = slot 1, the bottom row (5–8) = slot 2. The header shows both, for example T1 GRID FX 1:FILTER 2:RING (the page number always shows: with long effect names GRID is left out, then the names get shorter, for example T1 FX 1:PHAS 2:FLAN); touching a knob tells which slot it belongs to. FILTER's TYPE shows LP, BP or HP. MIX is always the slot's last knob (4 and 8). DRIVE's third knob is a bell EQ after the drive: EQ.F picks the frequency (60 Hz–12 kHz), SHIFT + EQ.G sets its gain (−12 to +12 dB, middle = 0). CRUSH has FILTER, BITS, RATE: FILTER is low-pass to the left, open in the middle, high-pass to the right; it filters only what the crush adds, the clean sound stays untouched (low-pass softens the sharp edges, fully left leaves almost the clean sound; high-pass leaves only the fine grit on top); BITS = fewer bits to the right, RATE = a lower sample rate to the right, SHIFT + RATE = JITTER: the sample rate wobbles at random (0 = steady). A new track and INIT start with both slots OFF; the header then says GRID FX (EMPTY). Row 2 picks the type of slot 1 (DRIVE, FILTER, CRUSH, RING, RESO, DELAY, GRAIN, SPRING), row 3 the type of slot 2 (MAXIM, TUBE, FOLD, TAPE, PHASER, FLANGE, WIDE, TANK); hold a pad 1 s to put another effect on it, see GRID FX LIBRARY. Press the lit pad again for OFF: the slot stops taking in sound. DELAY, RESO, GRAIN and SPRING let their tail ring out first (a frozen GRAIN fades); the others stop at once. SPRING is a spring reverb (SIZE, DECAY, TONE, MIX); SHIFT + MIX = FDBK feeds the output back into the springs: a longer, louder, dirtier tail; in the last quarter it goes further, and fully open the springs ring by themselves (held by a limiter). MUTE or STOP silences it. A newly picked effect starts with its knobs in the middle of a safe range. Row 4: the sends, as on ENGINE. After the slots, EFOS Terminal evens out the level automatically (slowly, within −9 to +6 dB), so effects do not make a track jump in volume.

## 7 PERF

### PERFORM

| Knobs | DENS (break / fill, middle = off), PROB (the master probability for every hit of every track, 100 % = all play), CHAOS (per hit: the velocity varies, now and then a roll in the grid, 2 hits as 32nds or 4 hits as 64ths starting on the hit, or a short glitch; pitch drifts only a few cents, like an old synth; it works on all tracks like the other PERFORM knobs: hold one track pad in row 1 and turn = only that track), FILTER (low \< off \> high), DELAY, WASH (opens send A a little on every track and pushes its tail: a quick wash over everything; send B, the delay by default, is left alone, it has its own DELAY knob), COMP (push the master compressor), MAKEUP (the master make-up gain that goes with COMP, for all tracks: far left = AUTO, as loud as without the compressor, then 0 to +24 dB; the same knob as MAKEUP on the COMP page). The track volume is LEVEL on ENGINE page 1, knob 8. While audio runs the header shows the CPU load. |
| Pads | Row 2 = MUTATE SOUND, one pad per track (pad 3 = T3): the sound of that track changes a little (never its pitch), so a few presses give a clearly different sound. SHIFT + pad = only a new pattern for that track, the sound stays. The pad lights, the track is selected and the step row shows its pattern for two seconds. Row 3 = MUTATE per part: SOUND, TRIGGERS, PITCH, LENGTH, FX, PROB, CHAOS. Row 4 = DEFAULT in the same order: SOUND (the default kit, see INIT), TRIGGERS (calm), PITCH (tracks 5–8 RANGE 8, the others off), pad 4 = RANDOM LENGTH 4–128, FX (off), PROB (100 %), CHAOS (off), pad 8 = ALL back to default. Rows 3 and 4: without SHIFT the selected track, SHIFT = all tracks. One exception: SHIFT + pad 8 of row 4 = ALL RANDOM: every track, everything new at once: triggers (with a random ALGO), melody, sound, effects, PROB, CHAOS and the length (4, 8, 16, 32 or 64), and here also ATTACK, DECAY, CURVE, LEVEL, PAN and PITCH, which random leaves alone everywhere else. The engines stay. UNDO takes it back. After a press the bottom line shows the new value of the selected track, for example T1 LENGTH 32, T1 PROB 65% or T1 FX DRIVE + RING; with SHIFT it starts with ALL:. UNDO takes any of these back. REPEAT is on TRIGGERS row 4. |
| Steps | The sequencer of the selected track, as on every page: steps and track length work here too. |
| MASTER LENGTH | SHIFT + \< \> (or SHIFT + the arrow keys) on any PERFORM page: OFF, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars (1/16 = one step). Every track keeps its own length, but all start again together on each master boundary. Saved with the state. Because of this, NUDGE (SHIFT + arrow keys) does not work on PERFORM. |

### PERFORM 2

| SWING | Knob 1: every second 16th comes later, for all tracks. OFF (50 %, straight) to 75 %. |
| MAIN.S, MAIN.R | Knobs 5 and 6: the MAIN key. It overrules the SCALE and ROOT of every track with RANGE above 0. MAIN.S fully left = OFF: each track plays its own SCALE and ROOT (PITCH 2). While MAIN is on, synth tracks with RANGE OFF snap to the nearest note of the key too. |

### SEND A and SEND B

| T1 SND, T1 PRE | The only two knobs of this track (the label shows its number): how much of it goes to the send, and PRE = before the track level (level 0 then leaves only the wet sound). |
| TYPE, 4 knobs, RETURN | Shared by all tracks (the header says SEND A PLATE: ALL); changing them changes the send for every track: ROOM (SIZE, DAMP, TONE, WIDTH: a room, after Freeverb; SIZE at the top = FREEZE), HALL (SIZE, DECAY, TONE, MOD: a big wide hall, a port of Airwindows Galactic; MOD detunes it), PLATE (the plate reverb of earlier versions), SPRING (see below), SHIMMER (SIZE, DECAY, SHIMMER, TONE: the plate with an octave up fed back into it, through the pitch shifter of Clouds; SHIMMER = how much), CLOUDSEED (SIZE, DECAY, MOD, TONE: a lush, long reverb, a port of the Schwung CloudSeed module; TONE cuts the highs, it comes 3 ms later than the other send), DELAY, TAPE (SHIFT + FDBK = FILTER on the repeats: low-pass left, high-pass right, open in the middle; each repeat gets darker or thinner), BBD, PHASER, FLANGE, CHORUS, LOOP (a driven feedback loop, like a wild tape echo), GRAIN (SIZE, DENS, PITCH, TEX; Clouds with a little reverb, wide stereo, one cloud for every track that sends to it; WASH on PERF raises its FEEDBACK when it sits in send A), SPRING (SIZE, INTENS, TONE, DRIVE: a tank of 3 springs of different length, driven harder for more boing; SIZE = the length of the springs (20 to 200 ms); TONE = left dark, right bright; INTENS = INTENSITY: the decay up to 85 %, above that the tank feeds back and fully open it runs away, held by saturation, like TAPE; WASH raises it in send A). |
| TONE (PLATE) | Works only on what goes into the reverb: left = low-pass down to 300 Hz, middle = OPEN, right = high-pass up to 3 kHz. You hear it only in the reverb. |

Pads: only row 4, the same send pads as on ENGINE: pads 1–4 SEND A at 5, 10, 20 or 35 %, pads 5–8 SEND B; pads 4 and 8 have their own colour and their own value: hold one and turn knob 1 to set it (0–100 %, the screen shows it; SHIFT = all tracks), it is kept per track and in the preset, and the pad changes colour when it is not the default 35 %; pad 1 or 5 again while lit = that send off (pad 1 white); the other pads only set their level, so SHIFT + the same pad puts every track on it. Selected track, SHIFT = all tracks. Rows 2 and 3 are empty here.

### COMP

The master compressor: TYPE (GLUE, PUNCH, SMASH), THRESH, RATIO (fully right = LIMIT), ATK, SAT, REL (release), MAKEUP and MIX on knob 8. MAKEUP fully left = AUTO (the compressed bus as loud as the dry bus), turned up = 0 to +24 dB by hand. The bar at the top shows a meter next to COMP: how much it compresses, 0 to 20 dB. The sum of the tracks and sends reaches the master FX and the compressor 6 dB down (headroom), and is brought back up at the very end, so COMP and SAT do not saturate at once.

### ALL ENGINE, ALL TRIGGERS

One parameter for all eight tracks: pad row 2 picks the parameter, the knobs show it for each track. SHIFT + a knob moves all tracks by the same amount. With nothing picked the bottom line reads ROW 2 PADS: PICK THE VALUE.

On ALL ENGINE, row 4 pad 2 picks VAR.D: decay variation for each track (knob 1 = T1 ... knob 8 = T8). Unlike the one-shot random of row 3, VAR.D keeps changing the decay on every hit, like VAR.S does for the sound.

Pad row 3 gives the picked parameter a random value, full range, on the track above it (pad 5 = T5); SHIFT + a row 3 pad gives every track its own random value. Row 4 pad 1 sets every parameter of the page for all tracks at random, in safe ranges, DECAY stays as it is: LEVEL 80–120, FILTER never fully closed, DRIVE and NOISE below the top; on ALL TRIGGERS the variations stay below half, PROB above 60 %, DENS in the middle, LENGTH 4–128 and CLOCK /2, 1 or X2. UNDO takes both back.

## 8 Presets

A preset is a snapshot of everything you hear: all eight tracks (sounds, patterns, melodies, mutes, effects), the sends, PERF (with scale and compressor) and the recordings. The tempo, the master volume and solo are never stored: loading a preset keeps your tempo. There are 64 slots.

![PRESETS screen](presets.png)

### Reading the screen

| Top left | PRESETS. |
| Top right | What the jog does: LOAD, or SAVE on an empty slot. While the PRESET LOOPER runs: LOOP 2/3. |
| List | The slots. The highlighted one is the slot you are on; ---- = empty. The **number in an inverted block** = the active preset: the one you last loaded (also PATTERNS ONLY or SOUNDS ONLY), last saved, or the one the PRESET LOOPER plays; it stays after switching off or an update. \* after its name = you changed something since it was loaded or saved (the tempo does not count). After INIT (SHIFT + DEL) the active block and the cursor go to the first empty slot. Press the jog on the active preset again: OVERWRITE? with NO / YES (the jog picks, a press confirms, \< cancels); YES saves what you hear now over it, for quick small changes. "\>" on the right = the slot the PRESET LOOPER plays. |
| Bottom line | What happened or what the jog will do: SAVED 02, LOADED 02, EMPTY SLOT, PRESS JOG: SAVE, NEXT BAR: NAME, … |

### Opening

MENU → PRESETS, or SHIFT + MENU. \< goes back.

### Actions: hold SHIFT

Hold SHIFT: a list of actions fills the screen: LOAD, PATTERNS ONLY, SOUNDS ONLY, OVERWRITE, RENAME, INIT, PRESET LOOPER, SONG MODE, CLEAN UP, DELETE. Keep SHIFT held, turn the jog to an action and press the jog: it is done at once on the slot you were on (its number is in the header). Let go of SHIFT without pressing and nothing happens. OVERWRITE, CLEAN UP and DELETE ask for a second press. Without SHIFT the jog press always does LOAD (SAVE on an empty slot).

### Save

1. Turn the jog to an **empty** slot (----).
2. Press the jog. The slot gets a random name and the bottom line says SAVED.

To save **over** an existing preset: hold SHIFT, turn the jog to OVERWRITE and press the jog twice. The whole screen then says SAVED with the preset's number and name for a moment (also after OVERWRITE? YES).

**INIT** in the action list starts a fresh INIT patch (as SHIFT + DEL) and saves it at once under a random name in an empty slot: the slot you are on when it is empty, otherwise the next empty one. It never saves over a preset. That slot becomes the active preset, so after your changes OVERWRITE saves them there. The screen shows NEW PRESET 05 and its name for a moment; with no empty slot left it says so and does nothing.

### Load

1. Turn the jog to a filled slot.
2. Press the jog. While playing, the preset comes in on the next bar (the bottom line says NEXT BAR); when stopped, at once.

Changed your mind? UNDO brings back what you had.

### Load only a part

| LOAD | The whole preset. This is what you want almost always. |
| PATTERNS ONLY | Only rhythms and melodies (and the mutes); your current sounds stay. |
| SOUNDS ONLY | Only sounds and effects; your current patterns stay. |

### PRESET LOOPER

Plays the saved presets one after another, each for a set number of bars, changing on the bar. Empty slots are skipped. Open it with LOOP, or hold SHIFT in PRESETS and pick PRESET LOOPER.

| Knob 1 FROM | The first slot. |
| Knob 2 TO | The last slot; fully left = ALL (every saved preset). FROM may be above TO. |
| Knob 3 BARS | How long each preset plays: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars. |
| Knob 4 REPEAT | How many rounds: 1X to 9X, fully right = ENDLESS. After the last round the looper stops and the last preset plays on. |
| Jog press | START / STOP. |

While it runs, the bottom line on every page shows LOOP 3/7 2 BARS ROUND 1/4 (the preset, the bars left of it, the round) with a bar that fills while the preset plays. \< stops it, on every page, before \< does anything else. SHIFT + LOOP starts and stops it from anywhere. Loading a preset by hand also stops it. The settings are kept with your presets.

### Delete

Quickest: hold DELETE (the X button), put the jog on the slot and press the jog twice: the first press asks, the second empties the slot. Or put the jog on the slot, hold SHIFT, turn the jog to DELETE and press twice. UNDO right after brings it back.

### Clean up

RENAME gives the preset on the slot a name of your own. A list comes up: INTRO, VERSE, CHORUS, BRIDGE, SOLO, BREAK, BUILD, DROP, OUTRO; turn the jog and press it to take that name (a name that is already used gets a number: VERSE 2). The last row, TYPE, spells a name letter by letter, up to 14: turn the jog for the letter, press it for the next one, \< when you are done (it saves the name). \< in the list goes back without renaming.

SONG MODE plays your presets as a song, in any order: a list of rows, each row one preset played a number of times. Turn the jog to a row; knob 1 picks its PRESET, knob 2 how many TIMES (1 to 16; also SHIFT + turn the jog), knob 3 how many BARS each time (1, 2, 4, 8, 16). Knob 8 is LOOP for the whole song: ON = after the last row it starts again at the top; OFF = the song stops after the last row and that last preset keeps playing (like the PRESET LOOPER). **Hold SHIFT** to see what the knobs do: the screen shows PRESET, TIMES and BARS of the row you are on and LOOP on knob 8; let go and the list is back. The bottom line also reminds you (K1 PRESET K2 TIMES K3 BARS), and touching a knob shows what it sets on that row. The last row, + ADD ROW, adds a row (a copy of the one above) and shows LOOP ON / OFF; DEL held + jog press deletes the row you are on. Press the jog on a row to start the song (from the top) or to stop it. \< stops the song and goes back to PRESETS in one press; on any other page \< also stops it, and SHIFT + LOOP does too. Each preset comes in on the bar, like the PRESET LOOPER. While it plays, the highlighted row follows the row that plays, PRESETS shows SONG 3/7 at the top and \< STOPS THE SONG at the bottom, and the bottom line of the other pages says SONG 3/7 VERSE 1/2 \< STOPS: row 3 of 7, preset VERSE, its first of two times. The song is kept with your presets; CLEAN UP keeps the rows on their presets.

CLEAN UP moves all filled slots up so there are no empty slots between them, in the same order. Press the jog twice: the first press asks, the second does it.

### Screensaver

MENU \> SCREENSAVER sets the type (INTRO, STARS, BOOT) and the wait (OFF, 1, 5 or 25 MIN; 5 is the default). PREVIEW shows it at once. The music keeps playing; the first press or turn only wakes the screen and does nothing else (touching a knob or letting go does not wake it). Opening EFOS plays the INTRO once; any press or turn skips it.

### MIDI

MENU \> MIDI \> CLOCK OUT (OFF by default) sends MIDI clock to the USB-A port: START when EFOS plays, 24 clocks per quarter on EFOS's tempo, STOP when it stops. No clock goes out while EFOS itself follows an incoming clock. CLOCK SENT shows how many clocks the Move took (OK) and refused (X). The INPUT, OUTPUT and channel rows are for the web app; MIDI notes in and out are not on the Move yet. Following an incoming MIDI clock: see chapter 9.

MENU \> MIDI \> CONTROLLER is for a MIDI controller on the USB-A port, such as a Faderfox EC4 or E16:

- **DEVICE**: AUTO (default), EC4, E16 or OTHER. With AUTO, EC4 or E16, CC 1-16 on channel 1 are read as relative knobs (the E16 layout): CC 1-8 turn knobs 1-8 of the page you are on, CC 9-16 the same knobs with SHIFT. Note 16 on channel 1 is SHIFT. OTHER reads every CC as an absolute knob (0-127), for LEARN only.
- **E16**: plug it in and it works. With DEVICE on AUTO or E16, EFOS sends the E16 the message that puts it in this layout (ENTER REMOTE MODE) every 2 seconds until it answers; the E16 row then shows CONNECTED.
- **EC4**: set it up once in Schwung (Global Settings \> Surfaces \> EC4 Setup), which writes the same layout into the EC4. After that it works with DEVICE on AUTO or EC4.
- **FOLLOW** (ON by default): the controller's 16 knobs follow the page on the screen, as above. OFF: only learned knobs do something.
- **LEARN**: ON, then touch a knob on the Move (with SHIFT if you want its SHIFT function) and turn a knob on the controller: that controller knob now always sets that knob, on that page, from any page. The screen shows LEARNED: CC 20 \> LEVEL. Turn LEARN OFF when you are done.
- **LEARNED**: the list of learned knobs. DEL + jog press removes one; CLEAR ALL removes them all (press twice).

Keep Schwung's own setting for the surfaces on CC ONLY, otherwise Schwung takes CC 1-16 for itself and EFOS does not see them.

## 9 On the Move

### Install and update

EFOS Terminal is a module for **Schwung**, the module host for Ableton Move. With Schwung installed, open the Schwung Manager in a browser on the same network: http://move.local:7700. Use **Install Custom Module** \> **From Tarball** with the file efos-module-\<version\>.tar.gz. Installing a newer file updates EFOS; your presets and settings stay.

### Open, park, quit

| Open | From Schwung's overtake modules: EFOS Terminal. Opening plays the INTRO once; any press or turn skips it. |
| \< (no menu open) | Parks EFOS: you are back on the Move and EFOS keeps playing. Open it again to carry on. When the PRESET LOOPER or SONG MODE runs, \< stops that first. |
| SHIFT + \< | Quits EFOS. The state is saved first. |
| Move's own set | When EFOS opens while Move's own set plays on Move's own clock, Move's transport is stopped, so only EFOS plays. |
| Schwung settings | Leave EFOS (\< or SHIFT + \<), then SHIFT + touch the volume knob + step 2. |

### MIDI clock in

When the Move follows an external MIDI clock (Move's MIDI sync setting), EFOS follows it 1:1: **Start** = play from step 1, **Stop** = stop and back to step 1. Steps stay locked to the clock. The tempo is measured over the last 4 beats and held until the clock really changes, so synced delays and LFOs do not warble. PLAY on EFOS while a clock runs (no Start seen) starts at once and stays on the clock. Without an external clock, EFOS's own TEMPO leads. MIDI clock out and a controller on USB-A: chapter 8, MIDI.

### Storage

Settings, presets and the last state are files in /data/UserData/schwung/efos/ on the Move; sample recordings in /data/UserData/schwung/efos/recordings/. They survive module updates. The last state is written every 10 seconds and shortly after every preset SAVE.

### Not on the Move yet

MIDI notes in and out, and preset files (LOAD, SAVE, EXPORT and IMPORT, which the web app has; on the Move they are left out of the menus). Presets in the 64 slots work and are kept on the Move.

EFOS Terminal never goes silent on purpose: runaway sounds are reset on their own, and the state is saved every few seconds. Where a value is out of range, the engine keeps playing.

Source and releases: https://github.com/screechpowers/EFOS-Terminal.
